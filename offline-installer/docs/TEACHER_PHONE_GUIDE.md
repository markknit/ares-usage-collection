# ARES Sync - Teacher Installation and Use

ARES Sync helps ARES Education receive scheduled usage summaries from your school's ARES server. After setup, it normally works automatically and does not need daily attention.

## Before you start

Ask your **ARES Advocate or ARES technical-support contact** for your school's one-time installation key. The app may call this an **enrollment code**. It looks like `XXXX-XXXX`, works once, and should not be shared with another person or phone.

You will need:

- an Android phone;
- access to your school's ARES or ARES2 network;
- the one-time installation key from ARES staff; and
- normal Internet or mobile data when enrollment needs to contact the central ARES service.

## Install ARES Sync at school without Internet

1. Connect your phone to the school's **ARES** or **ARES2** network.
2. In Chrome, open:

   `http://ares.local/app_install/`

3. Tap **Download ARES Sync for Android**.
4. Open `ares-sync.apk` from the download notification or Chrome's Downloads list.
5. If Android says Chrome or Files is not allowed to install apps, open the offered Settings page and enable **Allow from this source** for that app, then return to the installer.
6. If Android recommends an app scan, allow it and continue after it passes.
7. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

The APK is stored on the local ARES school server, so downloading and installing it does **not** require an Internet connection.

## Connect the phone to your school

1. If the app needs normal Internet to verify enrollment, enable mobile data or connect to Internet-capable Wi-Fi before continuing.
2. Type part of your school name and select **Find school**.
3. Select the exact school name.
4. Enter the one-time installation key from your ARES Advocate.
5. Select **Enroll this phone**.
6. Allow ARES Sync to suggest Wi-Fi networks.
7. Allow **Nearby Wi-Fi devices**. Older Android versions may call this permission **Location**; ARES Sync does not use it to determine your physical location.
8. Approve ARES or ARES2 if Android asks.
9. Allow notifications.

## How to know setup worked

The app should show your correct school and a normal ready/up-to-date status. Show the screen to your ARES Advocate before finishing the installation visit.

## What happens after setup

You do not need to open ARES Sync every day.

On scheduled collection dates, ARES Sync obtains a small usage-summary file from the school's local ARES server. When normal Internet becomes available later, it sends any pending file securely to ARES Education.

ARES Sync does not read your messages, contacts, photos, documents, or browsing history. It transfers the school's ARES usage-summary file.

## Important

- Keep ARES Sync installed.
- Keep notifications allowed.
- Do not share an installation key.
- Do not clear the app's storage unless ARES support instructs you to do so.
- A new phone or reinstallation requires a new installation key.
- If you need to reinstall the app while at school, reconnect to ARES/ARES2 and open `http://ares.local/app_install/`.

## When to contact your ARES Advocate

Ask for help if:

- you have not received an installation key;
- `http://ares.local/app_install/` does not open while connected to ARES/ARES2;
- Android will not install the downloaded APK;
- your school is missing or the wrong school is shown;
- enrollment fails after normal Internet is available; or
- the app repeatedly reports that it needs attention.
