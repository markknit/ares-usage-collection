## Source corrections and limits

- Enter at the remote school-name prompt now retains the current hostname;
  invalid names reprompt and EOF defers without a traceback or service changes.
- Preparation preserves existing remote service states and x11vnc console
  sharing. It does not reinstall active TigerVNC packages or replace binaries/
  unit/identity of active Tailscale. Activation remains a separate choice.
- Root AnyDesk CLI calls use root's HOME and omit graphical-session variables.
  A new helper selects the existing local X11 environment, repairs ownership
  only inside the user's profile, and launches the desktop client as ares.
  It preserves the new ID/password and detects an immediately aborting client.
  No unique X11 session or failed client launch leaves setup Pending.
- `ares-recover-anydesk-desktop` can attempt only that client recovery without
  another identity reset or display-manager restart. Successful process launch
  is explicitly not a successful unattended connection test.
