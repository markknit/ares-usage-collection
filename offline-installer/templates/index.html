🎯 Coverage

The initial delivery covers the full scope of Grade 10 STEM topics: Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Math Fundamentals, and General Science.

Future releases are planned to add Grades 11 and 12 STEM lesson plans and later expand to both STEM and non-STEM subjects across additional grades.

🔬 Methodology

The STEM lesson-plan templates were carefully authored with teaching staff at Seavuria Schools in Taita Taveta County, with production tools provided and managed by ARES Education.

The lessons are designed around Kenyan curriculum expectations and internationally recognized STEM teaching practices. They emphasize student engagement, inquiry, self-discovery, collaboration, and practical classroom use, while being localized to a Kenyan context.

🛠️ Editing and local adaptation

The Lesson Plan Editor allows schools and teachers to adapt or extend the supplied lesson plans where appropriate. A school administrator can control editing rights by subject and document.

For step-by-step instructions covering teacher, editor, subject-grade administrator, and site-administrator functions, open the Lesson3 User Guide (PDF).

We hope these resources make lesson preparation easier and classroom learning more engaging.
