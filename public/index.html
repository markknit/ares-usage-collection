What happens after setup

1. Reminder

ARES Sync reminds the teacher when a collection is due.

2. School collection

The teacher connects the phone to ARES or ARES2 and returns to ARES Sync. The usage report downloads to the phone.

3. Automatic submission

When normal internet access returns, ARES Sync sends the pending report automatically, even if the app is not open.
